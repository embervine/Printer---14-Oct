Privacy policy

Information about how the Embervine website works and what information you may choose to share.

Effective: October 14, 2026

Information you provide

When you choose to contact us, you may provide a name, email address, and information about your printer issue. Our website contact form opens your own email client; it does not send details directly to an Embervine server.

Third-party live chat

This website uses Tawk.to for live chat. If you use that chat, the service may process chat messages, technical data, and cookies under its own privacy practices. Please review Tawk.to's policy for details. Avoid sharing passwords, bank information or security codes.

External resources

Our pages may load Google Fonts and images provided by Unsplash. These services can receive technical connection details such as an IP address in order to deliver their resources.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our contact page or live chat. This policy should be reviewed and adapted to reflect your actual business processes before public launch.
