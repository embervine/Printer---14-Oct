QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

A little clarity goes a long way.

Answers to common questions about printer troubleshooting, independent support, and the ways we work.

Are you official support for Canon, HP or Epson?

No. Embervine is an independent service provider. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or authorized by Canon, HP, Epson, Brother, or other printer manufacturers.

Can you help with different printer brands?

We can discuss symptoms involving a wide range of printers. Specific repair options depend on the model, problem and availability of applicable service resources.

Can you help if my printer keeps showing offline?

Yes. An offline printer can be related to Wi-Fi, device settings, connection status or print queues. We can help walk through relevant troubleshooting checks.

Do you install or update printer drivers?

We can guide you through identifying appropriate printer software from official manufacturer sources. We do not represent manufacturer support or distribute proprietary drivers.

Does Embervine provide remote support throughout the US?

Remote troubleshooting is offered to customers across the United States, subject to support availability and the nature of the issue.

Can someone come to my location?

On-site service options vary by geographic area and technical requirements. Please contact us to ask about your specific location.

What should I prepare before speaking with support?

It helps to know your printer model, what error appears, what device you are printing from, and whether the issue began after a recent change.

Will every printer issue be fixable?

Not every printer can be repaired. Some hardware damage, aging components, warranty cases, or discontinued models may require replacement or manufacturer service.

Is it safe to share my password with support?

Do not share passwords, verification codes, or sensitive financial details. If remote access is ever considered, only proceed with informed consent and use trusted security practices.
