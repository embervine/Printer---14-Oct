What types of printer problems can you help with?

We provide troubleshooting guidance for setup, connectivity, print quality, software, paper feed, scanning, and various other printer concerns. Some repairs depend on printer condition and parts availability.

Is support available remotely?

Yes. Remote assistance may be suitable for printer setup, connection and software issues across the United States.

Do you offer in-person printer service?

In-person service may be available in selected areas. Contact our team to check availability for your location and situation.

Are you affiliated with printer manufacturers?

No. Embervine is an independent provider, not affiliated with or authorized by Canon, HP, Epson, or any other printer manufacturer.
